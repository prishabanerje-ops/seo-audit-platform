Critical UX problems found (independent of the coverage gap)

AuditCategory URL list — no sort, no filter, no search. 1000+ issues = manual scan.
IssueDetail prev/next — sibling fetch is silent; if it fails, pagination breaks with no error shown.
Health score formula — based only on 7 working categories. Shows "95/100" even though Images, Mobile, Speed, Content are completely unchecked.
AI recommendation UX — no distinction between cached (instant) vs streamed (slow). No undo on feedback.
