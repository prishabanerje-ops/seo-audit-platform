B — Expandable category tree

Sidebar shows "Issues by Category" as a collapsible tree. Active categories show severity dot. Unimplemented show hollow dot. Compare lives alongside it.

Pros

  • All 20 categories discoverable from sidebar
  • Severity dots show health at a glance

Cons

  • Sidebar gets tall with 20 items expanded
  • More complex to implement

Collapse all audit views (Overview, Categories, Compare) into a single "Audit Dashboard" sidebar item with internal tabs. Sidebar stays clean. Compare is always visible.

Pros

  • Cleanest sidebar
  • Compare always discoverable
  • Scales well as more views are added

Cons

  • Deep-links to categories break (bookmarks)
  • Biggest refactor of the three
